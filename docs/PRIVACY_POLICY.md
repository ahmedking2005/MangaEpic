# Privacy Policy — Manga Epic

Effective date: 19 November 2026

Manga Epic is a free Android manga reader. This policy explains what data the
app handles, what leaves your device, and your choices.

## Data that stays on your device

Your library, favourites, reading history, reading progress, bookmarks,
downloads and app settings are stored locally on your device. We have no
access to them unless you explicitly use an optional cloud feature described
below.

## Data collected automatically (Firebase)

The app includes Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics by Google:

- **Analytics** collects pseudonymous usage statistics: device model,
  Android version, app version, screens viewed and feature usage, and an
  app-instance identifier. It does not contain your name, email, library or
  reading content.
- **Crashlytics** collects crash reports when the app fails: stack traces,
  device model, Android version and app state at the time of the crash. Crash
  reports do not include your library, history or downloaded files.

This data is processed by Google under Google's privacy policy and is used
only to fix bugs and improve the app.

## Manga content

Manga pages are loaded directly from the third-party sources you choose
inside the app. Manga Epic does not host, store or proxy manga content or
images on its own servers.

## Accounts and cloud sync (optional, when offered)

If the app offers an optional account in the future, signing in will sync
only the data needed for that feature (such as favourites, history and
reading progress) to our Supabase backend, protected by row-level security
so that each user can access only their own data. Sync is off unless you
sign in. You may request deletion of your account data at any time via the
contact below.

## Your choices

- Clear all local data at any time from Android Settings → Apps →
  Manga Epic → Storage → Clear data, or by uninstalling the app.
- Analytics collection can be limited system-wide via your device's
  advertising/privacy settings.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect
personal information from them.

## Changes

Material changes to this policy will be noted in the app release notes and
in this document's history in the project repository.
